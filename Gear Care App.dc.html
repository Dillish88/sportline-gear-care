Calls I made

  • No sign-in. Phone number is the identity; the job link is the account.
  • Own rider is free inside 3 km of the shop and is offered first — it is the cheapest leg you control.
  • Partner fares are shown per leg, twice, labelled estimate. The ₹40–90 tiers in the repo are placeholders until real trips are logged.
  • Pay on delivery stays the default; no gateway in this pass.
  • Completion message is staff-triggered, pre-typed. Automatic sending needs the Business Platform.
  • Label is 50 × 25 mm thermal: job code, service, spec, customer, and a QR that reopens the job card.

Open questions

  • Real Porter / Rapido fares for the three shops — five logged trips would replace every estimate here.
  • GST on services is still 0% in the till. A public page quoting ₹1,200 with pickup is a bigger exposure than a ₹100 oiling.
  • Purasaiwalkam parked until the address and number exist. Two shops in the app for now.
  • Label printer: sized for a 50 mm direct-thermal roll. Zebra ZD230 or TSC TE200 class.
