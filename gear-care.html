How it works

Four steps, door to door

01

A rider picks up from your door. Photographed before it moves.

03

04

It comes back

Returned to your door. Pay by UPI or cash on delivery.

Pickup and return are two separate delivery trips, so the delivery cost applies both ways. On a single racket that is a real share of the bill — dropping it at the shop yourself is always cheaper, and usually faster.

Playing tonight?

On a quiet weekday everything goes out in an hour or two anyway, so there is nothing to pay for. When the bench is backed up — most Saturdays and Sundays — you can pay to jump the queue. Ask at the counter or on WhatsApp and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth it today.
